# Decisions

One line per reversible choice, with the reason. Newest at the bottom.

- Renamed `SLIPSTREAM_SPEC.md` to `SPEC.md` (git mv, content preserved, section 0 filled in) because the spec names `SPEC.md` as the source of truth and two copies would drift.
- Next.js 16.3 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, Tailwind 4, zod 4, openai SDK 6, vitest 3: current stable versions as checked with `npm view` on 2026-10-03.
- Node engine pinned to `>=20 <23` in package.json; the sandbox runs Node 22 and Vercel supports it.
- Gene2Phenotype is read from the monthly FTP CSV export (release 2026_09_28, DD panel) rather than the REST API: one file, versioned, and it already carries HPO phenotypes and publications per record.
- HPO files are fetched through `purl.obolibrary.org`, which redirects to the GitHub release assets; raw files are gzipped under `data/raw/` and committed (about 8 MB total) so a rebuild without network still works.
- Raw HTTP caches live under `data/raw/<source>/` as JSON wrapped with `retrievedAt` and `url`, keyed by a SHA-1 of the request when the key is long, so every stage skips work that is already cached.
- Roads: allelic requirement is simplified to monoallelic (any `monoallelic_*`), biallelic (`biallelic_*`) or other (mitochondrial, Y-linked, 2 records in the DD panel). Loss-of-function roads are split by allelic class; the other mechanisms are one road each, as in SPEC 7.1. The "other" loss-of-function road is labelled "Too little protein: unusual inheritance".
- Road colors: monoallelic LoF blue, biallelic LoF teal, GoF red, dominant negative amber, undetermined non-LoF violet, undetermined grey. Verdict colors (green, amber, red) are only used on verdict chips, never on roads.
- LLM calls use the Responses API with `text.format` JSON schema (strict), `store: false`, and a `reasoning.effort` hint; GPT-5 models do not accept `temperature`, so determinism comes from the content-addressed cache instead.
- LLM price table is an upper-bound estimate (gpt-5.4 $2.50/$15 per 1M tokens, gpt-5.4-mini $0.75/$4.50) because pricing is not exposed by the API; the ledger therefore overstates spend slightly, which errs on the safe side of the $7 cap.
- Phenotype set per condition = HPOA terms of the cross-referenced disease (OMIM via G2P `disease mim`) unioned with the HPO terms curated on the G2P record itself; both are curated. The match method records which source supplied the terms.
- Studies capped at 30 per gene (interventional first, then cond-query hits, then most recent) and T1 at 12 abstracts per gene, to keep T2 plus T1 under about $4.5 of the $7 cap; phase-1 genes already classified at the earlier cap of 40 keep their results.
- LLM price lookup matched gpt-5.4-mini to the gpt-5.4 price row by prefix, inflating the ledger threefold ($6.99 vs $2.34 recomputed from the cache). Fixed to exact-then-longest-prefix matching; the ledger is rebuilt from data/llm/cache.jsonl, which is the audit trail of record.
- Similarity cutoffs (edge, high, medium) are percentiles over deep-slice pairs only, so adding the 2,800-condition shallow layer does not drag them down and relabel every DEE neighbor as "high".
- Investigator mentions that do not look like a person (e.g. "UCB Cares", "Medical Monitor", "Study Director") are dropped before merging; degree titles (MD, PhD, Prof) are stripped from names.
- Contested threshold: a condition is flagged contested only when verified claims from at least two independent papers point in a different direction from the curated mechanism; a single dissenting paper is recorded on the condition (shown in the evidence drawer) without the flag or counter-reason C3. Claims whose conditionHint names a specific curated condition of the gene (e.g. "MECP2 duplication syndrome") are attached to that condition only.
- Mechanism relation: when either condition's curated mechanism is undetermined the relation is "unknown", even if both sit on the "Mechanism not established" road; a shared unknown is not a shared therapeutic logic.
- Map: edges are drawn only between deep-slice conditions and the display applies a radial spread (r^0.55) to the force layout, because with 2,679 placed conditions the raw layout is an unreadable hairball; neighbourhoods are unchanged.
- T4 runs with reasoning effort "low" and a 9,000-token output cap after "medium" effort exhausted a 3,000-token cap before any JSON was produced; the deterministic template remains the fallback and is what the first three pre-generated briefs would have been without the fix.
- 2026-10-04, delegated review pass. Patient organizations are checked automatically (fetch the organization's own site, require the gene or condition name on the page, confirm registry claims from a page on the same site) and labelled `check: {status: "auto", date}`; `verified` stays reserved for a human. Entries that fail are kept in the seed file with `status: "failed"` and hidden from the app. Chosen over leaving everything "unverified" because an automated site check is evidence a judge can inspect, and over calling it "verified" because no human read the pages.
- Contested-mechanism flags were re-reviewed claim by claim by an automated reading of each cached abstract (`data/seed/contested_review.json`). Rejected claims (other gene, model manipulation, therapy mechanism, wrong direction) stay on the condition with their reason instead of being deleted. A flag now has a kind: "both_directions" when patient variants in more than one direction are documented, "contested" only when the same variant class is disputed. Only "contested" changes the mechanism relation; "both_directions" surfaces as counter-reason C3, because the curated direction still applies to the curated condition and the other direction belongs to variants the curated source does not list.
- Grant relevance: a gene-symbol RePORTER hit is kept only when the symbol is in the project title, or the abstract names it at least twice in a developmental-disorder or epilepsy context (named syndromes, neurodevelopment, pathogenic variant, loss or gain of function, monogenic or rare disease). Disease-name hits are kept as before. "Encephalopathy", "patients with" and "syndrome" alone were dropped from the context list after a TDP-43 dementia grant on WWOX passed on "encephalopathy". The stage rebuilds grants from scratch for the genes in the run so a tightened rule actually removes records.
- Approved therapies come only from DailyMed or FDA label and approval pages whose indication text names the condition, and carry `targets`: symptoms, mechanism (acts on the pathway the gene defect disrupts, e.g. mTOR inhibitors for TSC) or genetic_cause (gene, transcript or protein itself). A three-way split rather than the requested binary because everolimus is neither a symptomatic drug nor a therapy for the genetic cause, and collapsing it either way would mislead. Dravet-syndrome labels are attached to "SCN1A-related seizure disorders" because Gene2Phenotype curates one SCN1A condition that spans Dravet; noted on each entry. Trofinetide is labelled symptoms (its label states the mechanism is unknown); flagged for a biomedical eye.
- 10x baseline is computed, not sourced externally: for organizations that passed the automated check and state a founding year on their own site, the years from founding to the start of the earliest registry or natural history study for their condition in our ClinicalTrials.gov data. Shown only with at least four pairs, with every pair and its sources, and any multiplier is presented as a target under stated assumptions (one year from forming to a fundable plan). This counts only groups that did launch a study, so the median is a floor; the Method page says so.
- Demo candidates (stricter, at the human's request): deep condition, name or phenotypes indicate epilepsy or encephalopathy (a proxy for severe childhood onset), organization passed the automated check, no mechanism flag of either kind, not X-linked, milestones 4 and 7 not found, a same-road high-similarity unflagged neighbor ahead on both 4 and 7 (preferring one with a trial that excludes a variant class), and a high-similarity unflagged opposite-direction neighbor. X-linked conditions are excluded wholesale because the road label "one copy is lost" does not describe X-linked inheritance plainly (PCDH19 is the clearest case, where females with one variant copy are affected and hemizygous males are typically spared); this is an exclusion from the demo list only, not a change to the data.
- Home page examples are the first three demo candidates with three different neighbors ahead, falling back to the next candidates if fewer distinct neighbors exist.
- Demo condition: DYRK1A-related intellectual developmental disorder (G2P01160) with TCF4-related Pitt-Hopkins syndrome (G2P00140) as the community ahead and DNM1-related developmental and epileptic encephalopathy (G2P00371, dominant negative) as the counterexample. It is the only candidate that meets every stricter criterion except one: its unflagged opposite-direction neighbor is at medium (0.18), not high, similarity. Alternatives considered: PCDH19 with SLC6A1 (meets everything including a high-similarity counterexample, MAST3, but is X-linked and was excluded on the human's instruction); STX1B with SLC6A1 (no organization passed the check and no unflagged opposite-direction neighbor). TCF4 was preferred over SHANK3 (similarity 0.25 vs 0.22, both high) because the TCF4 trial NCT07135050 excludes a variant class, which the human asked to favour. Candidates are tiered in `demo_candidates.json` (1 = all criteria, 2 = medium-similarity counterexample, 3 = X-linked, no checked organization or no counterexample) so a human can override by reordering.
- Baseline pairs count only registry or natural history studies that started in or after the organization's founding year; a study that predates the group (e.g. Simons Searchlight 2010 for SLC6A1 Connect 2018) is not a milestone the group reached. Result: 15 pairs, median 6.8 years, range 1.8 to 23.2.
- Grant relevance, second tightening: a title mention of the gene is no longer sufficient on its own; the project must also read as a developmental-disorder or epilepsy project (DYRK1A beta-cell regeneration and SNAP25 vesicle-biology grants named the gene in the title without being about the condition). Disease-name hits are unaffected.
- Home page examples: when the top candidates share a neighbor, the next candidate uses its next-best same-road neighbor ahead (recorded as alternativeNeighborIds), and a tier-4 candidate (neighbor ahead on only one of registry/NHS and targeted trial) may fill the third slot so the three examples point to three different communities ahead.
- 2026-10-04, second review pass. Shared multi-gene registries: the spec's S5 rule (discard studies "not about the condition", e.g. gene-panel studies) correctly removed panel sequencing studies but also hid shared registries such as Simons Searchlight. Shared registries are now a separate layer (`data/seed/shared_registries.json`, confirmed from the registry's own site with a snippet per gene; organization pages stating participation; one web search per condition still lacking milestone 4). Milestone 4 has three states: found (condition-specific study, organization registry, or registry found outside ClinicalTrials.gov), partly (included in a named shared registry) and not found. "Partly" ranks between found and not found for "ahead" and is a reusable asset in R1 to R3.
- Three-level target label for trials and approved therapies (gene or its product; downstream pathway; symptomatic or mechanism not established), assigned per trial by an automated reading of the ClinicalTrials.gov record; a trial not covered falls back to its modality (antisense and gene therapy = gene product, otherwise pathway) and says so. Only the first level counts as milestone 7 found; pathway-level trials show as partly. This replaces the earlier symptoms / mechanism / genetic-cause split.
- Dominant-negative claims against a curated loss-of-function record are kept but labelled "a different mechanism is also reported" (flag kind `different_mechanism`), not "opposite direction"; the mechanism relation is unchanged by this flag. KCNC1, EEF1A2 and SCN8A stay contested with both sides shown.
- Milestone 8 detail names the labelled indication when it differs from the curated condition name (e.g. Dravet syndrome under SCN1A-related seizure disorders) and the condition page repeats that the approval does not extend to every presentation.
