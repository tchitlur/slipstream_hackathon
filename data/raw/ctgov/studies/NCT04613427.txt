Title: Predictors of Rupture Risk of Intracranial Aneurysms
Official title: Predictors of Rupture Risk of Intracranial Aneurysms - Impact of Lipid Metabolism and Mitochondrial Function
Status: COMPLETED. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Subarachnoid Hemorrhage, Aneurysmal; Intracranial Aneurysm
Interventions: none
Summary: Patients admitted to Haukeland University Hospital with either UIA or aSAH underwent a measurement of bioelectrical impedance and body mass composition using InBody 10. Lipids and lipoproteins were collected from plasma. 60 patients in total were included in the study.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Admitted electively for treatment of unruptured intracranial aneurysm
* Admitted acutely for aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage
* Informed consent form signed by patient or close relative

Exclusion Criteria:

* Pregnant
* Pacemaker
* Subarachnoid hemorrhage without identified aneurysmal rupture
* Unruptured aneurysm with previous SAH/intracranial hemorrhage
