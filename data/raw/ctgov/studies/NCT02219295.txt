Title: SEGATROM: Sensory and Gastrointestinal Impact of Taste Receptor Variants on Human Metabolism and Nutrition
Official title: Sensory and Gastrointestinal Impact of Taste Receptor Variants on Human Metabolism and Nutrition
Status: COMPLETED. Type: INTERVENTIONAL. Phases: NA.
Conditions: Disorder of Glucose Regulation
Interventions: sweet block (DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT); bitter block (DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT); umami-block (DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT); gastroscopy (OTHER)
Summary: We investigate the impact of taste receptors (sweet, bitter and umami) on human metabolism. There ist growing evidence, that components of the taste signalling system, including the taste receptor proteins , have important functions in the regulation of metabolic parameters in non-gustatory tissues.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Healthy persons

Exclusion Criteria:

* Tumor in history
* Pregnancy
* Severe illness in history
* Bodymass index below 18
