Title: Evaluation of Clinical Signs and Symptoms of Chronic Heart Failure in Patients Treated With Candesartan Cilexetil in Croatia
Official title: Evaluation of Clinical Signs and Symptoms of Chronic Heart Failure in Patients Treated With Candesartan Cilexetil in Croatia
Status: COMPLETED. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Chronic Heart Failure
Interventions: none
Summary: The purpose of this study is to determine whether treatment with candesartan cilexetil for at least 6 months in patients with chronic heart failure improves clinical signs and symptoms of chronic heart failure.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients with diagnosed chronic heart failure
* Patients treated for at least 6 months with candesartan cilexetil
* Signed Informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:

* Hypersensitivity to candesartan cilexetil
* Pregnancy / lactation
* Severe hepatic impairment
