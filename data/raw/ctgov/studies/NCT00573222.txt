Title: Non-interventional Surveillance of Effectiveness of Symbicort® Maintenance And Reliever Therapy (Symbicort® SMART) in the Treatment of Moderate and Severe Asthma
Official title: Non-interventional Surveillance of Effectiveness of Symbicort® Maintenance And Reliever Therapy (Symbicort® SMART) in the Treatment of Moderate and Severe Asthma
Status: COMPLETED. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Asthma
Interventions: Symbicort (DRUG)
Summary: This is a 3-month non-interventional, observational study on patients with moderate and severe asthma.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients with moderate and severe asthma on Symbicort SMART treatment

Exclusion Criteria:

* Under 18 years old
