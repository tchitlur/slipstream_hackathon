Title: A Cohort Study on the Prognosis of Neonatal KCNQ2 Gene-associated Epileptic Encephalopathy
Official title: A Cohort Study on the Prognosis of Neonatal KCNQ2 Gene-associated Epileptic Encephalopathy
Status: UNKNOWN. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Seizures; Seizure Disorder; Seizure Newborn; Seizures, Generalized; Epileptic Encephalopathy; Epileptic Encephalopathy, Neonatal-onset; Epileptic Encephalopathy, Infant-onset; KCNQ2
Interventions: KCNQ2 (GENETIC)
Summary: The researchers hope to explore the etiological distribution and influencing factors of KCNQ2-related neonatal convulsions or refractory epileptic encephalopathy, and to improve the level of assessment, identification, intervention and shunt of KCNQ2-related convulsions. To formulate countermeasures and measures for prevention, management and health education.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Primary or initial convulsion
* Postnatal age \<28 days.
* Seizure in the neonatal period
* Informed consent of parents

Exclusion Criteria:

* Seizure caused by congenital cerebral hypoplasia or multiple structural malformations.
* Seizure caused by other system-related syndromes.
* Seizure caused by perinatal or postpartum factors such as HIE, infection, intracranial hemorrhage, etc.
