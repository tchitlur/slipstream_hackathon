Title: Paroxysmal Atrial Fibrillation: Role of Inflammation, Oxidative Stress Injury and Effect of Statins
Official title: Paroxysmal Atrial Fibrillation: Role of Inflammation, Oxidative Stress Injury and Effect of Statins
Status: COMPLETED. Type: INTERVENTIONAL. Phases: PHASE2.
Conditions: Atrial Fibrillation
Interventions: Simvastatin (DRUG); Placebo (DRUG)
Summary: The purpose of this study is to determine the effect of statin therapy for prevention of atrial fibrillation (AF) in pacemaker and non-pacemaker patients with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the absence of significant coronary artery disease.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients with paroxysmal AF (\> 3 epis each \> 15 min in length) over 6 months
* Patients on stable antiarrhythmic drug therapy and life expectancy \> 1 year

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patients with PAF due to reversible cause
* Chronic inflammatory conditions
* Other medical conditions requiring statin therapy
* Patients on amiodarone or verapamil
* Elevated CK or ALT
* Life expectancy \<1 year
* TAVN ablation
* Geographic isolation
* Inability to give informed consent
