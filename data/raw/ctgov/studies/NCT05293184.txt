Title: The Global Angelman Syndrome Registry
Official title: The Global Angelman Syndrome Registry
Status: RECRUITING. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Angelman Syndrome
Interventions: Observational study only (OTHER)
Summary: The Global Angelman Syndrome Registry is an online patient organisation driven registry to collect information about the natural history of children and adults with Angelman Syndrome. The registry will facilitate 1) recruitment for clinical trials into therapies and interventions to benefit participants with Angelman Syndrome and their families, and 2) advancement of research and best standards of care for Angelman Syndrome.

The registry is currently available in English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, Italian, Polish, Hindi, and Brazilian Portuguese.
