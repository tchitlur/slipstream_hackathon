Title: Studies of the Variable Phenotypic Presentations of Rapid-Onset Dystonia Parkinsonism and Other Movement Disorders
Official title: Clinical, Genetic, and Cellular Consequences of Mutations in the NA,K-ATPase ATP1A3
Status: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Dystonia; Parkinsonism
Interventions: none
Summary: The purposes of this study are to identify persons with rapid-onset dystonia-parkinsonism (RDP) or mutations of the RDP gene, document prevalence of the disease, and map its natural history.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* clinical presentation consistent with ATP1A3 disease (RDP, AHC) or confirmed diagnosis of RDP or AHC

Exclusion Criteria:

* none
