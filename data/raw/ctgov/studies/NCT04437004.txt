Title: Treatment of Dravet Syndrome With Fenfluramine (Expanded Access Protocol)
Official title: Treatment of Dravet Syndrome With Fenfluramine (Expanded Access Protocol)
Status: APPROVED_FOR_MARKETING. Type: EXPANDED_ACCESS. Phases: none.
Conditions: Dravet Syndrome
Interventions: Fenfluramine (DRUG)
Summary: The purpose of this research study is to (1) provide access to fenfluramine for patients with intractable epilepsy associated with Dravet syndrome, and (2) evaluate the safety of fenfluramine.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Genetic diagnosis of Dravet syndrome with intractable seizures despite failing all available medications for seizures.
