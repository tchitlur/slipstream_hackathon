Title: Effects of Adherence to a Mediterranean Diet Style on Cardiovascular Risk
Official title: Effects of Adherence to a Mediterranean Diet Style on Cardiovascular Risk by Means Its Influence on Serum Levels of Ceramides and on Some Atherothrombotic Candidate Gene Expression
Status: COMPLETED. Type: INTERVENTIONAL. Phases: NA.
Conditions: Cardiovascular Risk Factor
Interventions: low fat (BEHAVIORAL); mediterranean diet (BEHAVIORAL)
Summary: Evaluation of the effect of adherence to Mediterranean Diet on some surrogate vascular markers and on gene expression of some cardiometabolic genes
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* at least two of the following eligibility criteria:

  1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus
  2. Arterial hypertension
  3. BMI ≥ 25
  4. Active smoking
  5. Family history of early cardiovascular disease
  6. Previous cardiovascular or cerebrovascular events (\> 6 months)

Exclusion Criteria:

* All patients with recent (\<6 months) cardiovascular or cerebrovascular events were excluded.
