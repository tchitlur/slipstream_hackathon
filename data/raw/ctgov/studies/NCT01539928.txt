Title: Dual PET/CT Imaging in Lung Cancer
Official title: Dual PET/CT Imaging in Lung Cancer (Danish Title: En og Tre Timers FDG-PET/CT Ved Lungecancer)
Status: UNKNOWN. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: Lung Cancer
Interventions: Dual FDG-PET/CT (OTHER)
Summary: The purpose of this study is to compare conventional PET/CT scan performed 1 hour after injection of the radioactive tracer FDG (PET/CT\[1\]) with PET/CT performed after 3 hours (PET/CT\[3\]) in a group of patients with biopsy verified lung cancer or high suspicion of lung cancer after initial work-up (chest x-ray and CT of thorax/upper abdomen), who are potentially operable.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Cytologic/histologic-verified lung cancer or high suspicion of lung cancer after initial work up
* Candidate to curative intended surgery after initial work up (usually chest x-ray, CT of chest/upper abdomen and if needed spirometry).

Exclusion Criteria:

* Former lung cancer
* Contraindications for PET/CT: Pregnancy, recent chemoradiotherapy
* Diabetes mellitus.
