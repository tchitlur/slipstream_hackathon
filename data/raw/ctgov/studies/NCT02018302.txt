Title: Post Study Continuation of C7 for G1D
Official title: Post Study Continuation of C7 for G1D
Status: NO_LONGER_AVAILABLE. Type: EXPANDED_ACCESS. Phases: none.
Conditions: Glut1 Deficiency Syndrome; Glucose Transporter Type 1 Deficiency Syndrome
Interventions: Triheptanoin (DRUG)
Summary: This protocol provides ongoing access to triheptanoin for patients who did complete an initial pilot protocol, and provides the opportunity to collect long-term safety data from patients treated with triheptanoin.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients enrolled in the pilot trial (UTSW 122010-186)

Exclusion Criteria:

* Subjects who did not complete the pilot trial (UTSW 122010-186)
