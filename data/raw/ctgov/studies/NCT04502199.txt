Title: Dysautonomic Phenotype in Male Patients With MECP2 Mutation
Official title: Dysautonomic Signs Among MECP2boys
Status: UNKNOWN. Type: OBSERVATIONAL. Phases: none.
Conditions: MECP2-Related Severe Neonatal Encephalopathy; Dysautonomia; Masculinity
Interventions: non interventional study (OTHER)
Summary: Dysautonomic signs are well known among girls with a Rett Syndrom. Rett syndrom is caused by a MECP2 mutation in 95% of cases. We want to search dysautonomic signs among boys with a MECP2 mutations because they are less studied than the girls and they have more varied phenotypes.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:less than 16 years old or dead before 16 years old patients of masculin sex with a missense or non-sens mutation in the MECP2 gene -

Exclusion Criteria:patient under judicial protection or participation refusal by one of the legal guardian
