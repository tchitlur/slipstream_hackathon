Title: Behavior Change and Maintenance Intervention for HIV+ MSM Methamphetamine Users
Official title: Behavior Change and Maintenance Intervention for HIV+ MSM Methamphetamine Users
Status: COMPLETED. Type: INTERVENTIONAL. Phases: PHASE2.
Conditions: HIV Infections; Substance Abuse; Sexually Transmitted Diseases
Interventions: EDGE counseling, no maintenance (BEHAVIORAL); EDGE counseling, plus maintenance (BEHAVIORAL); Attention control (BEHAVIORAL)
Summary: This study tests the effectiveness of a behavioral intervention to reduce sexual risk behavior in HIV-positive, methamphetamine-using men who have sex with men (MSM). It builds on the findings of a previous study (R01 DA012116, "Promoting safer sex in HIV+ homosexual and bisexual men who use methamphetamine"). That study achieved significant short-term results that eroded over time. Accordingly, this study hypothesizes that the addition of a maintenance component to the already proven counseling and educational components of the treatment model will result in longer-lasting positive effects.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* HIV+
* Used methamphetamine at least twice in last two months
* At least some recent sexual partners were male
* At least some recent sexual activity has been high-risk for transmission of HIV

Exclusion Criteria:

* Current major psychiatric diagnosis
* Only HIV+ sex partners in past two months
* Consistently protected sex with HIV- or serostatus-unknown partners in last two months
* Sexually inactive in last two months
* Found out about HIV+ status less than two months ago
