Title: Stiripentol in Dravet Syndrome
Official title: Compassionate Use of Stiripentol in Intractable Epilepsy Due to Dravet Syndrome
Status: NO_LONGER_AVAILABLE. Type: EXPANDED_ACCESS. Phases: none.
Conditions: Dravet Syndrome
Interventions: stiripentol (DRUG)
Summary: The patient has failed all other available agents and has intractable epilepsy due to Dravet Syndrome. Stiripentol is highly efficacious in Dravet Syndrome. The overall goals of therapy with Stiripentol are primarily to significantly reduce the frequency and severity of seizures.
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* Genetic diagnosis of Dravet syndrome with intractable seizures despite failing all available medications for seizures
