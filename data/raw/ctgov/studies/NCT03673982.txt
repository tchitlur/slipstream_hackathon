Title: Carer Skills Training for Inpatients With Anorexia Nervosa (iCASK)
Official title: Bridging Transitions From Hospital to Home: Collaborative Skill Sharing Intervention With Carers (iCASK)
Status: COMPLETED. Type: INTERVENTIONAL. Phases: NA.
Conditions: Anorexia Nervosa
Interventions: iCASK (BEHAVIORAL)
Summary: To improve treatment for patients with severe anorexia nervosa admitted for inpatient care, and to help their families. Inpatients and their families will be offered a novel intervention which includes multimedia training materials. These materials provide guidance in how families can provide support to maintain and build on changes made during inpatient care
Eligibility: Inclusion Criteria:

* aged 17 or over
* DSM-5 diagnosis of Anorexia Nervosa with a body mass index (BMI) of \< 18.5 kg/m2

Exclusion Criteria:

* Insufficient knowledge of English
* Severe mental or physical illness needing treatment in its own right (e.g. psychosis or diabetes mellitus)
