# Patient organization gaps (searched 2026-10-03; re-searched 2026-10-04, no organization confirmed)

Genes from `genes.txt` for which no patient organization's own site could be confirmed. One line per gene with what was searched. On 2026-10-04 each gene received exactly one additional WebSearch with the query `<GENE> patient foundation OR alliance OR family organization official site`; none returned a gene-specific organization on its own domain, so all 11 genes remain gaps.

- SCN3A — 2026-10-03: searched "SCN3A patient foundation official website" and "SCN3A foundation OR alliance families nonprofit"; results only returned SCN2A organizations and the Broad SCN-Portal (research tool, not a patient org). 2026-10-04: results were FamilieSCN2A Foundation pages (scn2a.org, NORD, CZI) only.
- SCN1B — 2026-10-03: searched "SCN1B patient foundation official website"; only Simons Searchlight (research program) and Dravet Syndrome Foundation genetics pages; no SCN1B-specific org. 2026-10-04: results were SCN2A/SCN8A organization pages and scn1agoffoundation.org (SCN1A gain-of-function, not SCN1B).
- KCNQ3 — 2026-10-03: searched "KCNQ3 patient foundation official website" and "site:kcnq2cure.org KCNQ3"; KCNQ2 Cure Alliance and Jack Pribaz Foundation sites focus on KCNQ2 and neither retrieved page states it serves KCNQ3 families. RIKEE (rikee.org) is a Baylor research database, not a patient org. 2026-10-04: results were kcnq2cure.org pages only; the kcnq2cure.org homepage mentions "KCNQ3" solely in the title of a linked Cambridge Elements book ("KCNQ2- and KCNQ3-Associated Epilepsy"), which is not evidence that the Alliance serves KCNQ3 families.
- HCN1 — 2026-10-03: searched "HCN1 epilepsy patient foundation official website" and "HCN1 Foundation OR HCN1 Epilepsy Foundation"; only general epilepsy foundations and journal articles. 2026-10-04: results were unrelated patient-advocacy organizations (Family Caregiver Alliance, Patient Health Alliance, AHCF); nothing HCN1-specific.
- STX1B — 2026-10-03: searched "STX1B patient foundation official website"; results were all STXBP1 organizations. 2026-10-04: results were again STXBP1 Foundation pages only.
- SYN1 — 2026-10-03: searched "SYN1 gene patient foundation official website"; results were all SYNGAP1 organizations. 2026-10-04: results were SYNGAP1 Foundation and CURE SYNGAP1 pages only.
- ARX — 2026-10-03: searched "ARX Foundation patient organization official website" and "ARX gene foundation families nonprofit epilepsy"; only pharmacy/health-tech companies named ARx and medical references. 2026-10-04: results were unrelated organizations (ATRX Research Alliance for ATR-X syndrome, The Arc Alliance, Arkansas Family Alliance); nothing for the ARX gene.
- ALDH7A1 — 2026-10-03: searched "ALDH7A1 pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy patient foundation official website"; found pdeonline.org (PDE Consortium, international registry) but the site describes itself as a clinician/researcher consortium, not a patient/family organization, so it was excluded. 2026-10-04: results were ALD Alliance (adrenoleukodystrophy) and Wikipedia gene pages; nothing ALDH7A1-specific.
- PNPO — 2026-10-03: searched "PNPO deficiency patient foundation official website"; only OMIM/MedlinePlus/GeneReviews references. 2026-10-04: results were generic patient-advocacy foundations (Patient Advocate Foundation, PAN Foundation, PKD Foundation); nothing PNPO-specific.
- DEPDC5 — 2026-10-03: searched "DEPDC5 patient foundation official website" and "DEPDC5 nonprofit family foundation alliance OR foundation OR cure"; only Child Neurology Foundation / Defeating Epilepsy Foundation disease pages. 2026-10-04: results were Child Neurology Foundation and GeneReviews disease pages, journal articles and a Dup15q Alliance DEE-P page; no DEPDC5-specific organization.
- EEF1A2 — 2026-10-03: searched "EEF1A2 foundation patient organization official website" and "EEF1A2 foundation OR nonprofit families"; no EEF1A2-specific organization found. 2026-10-04: results were generic patient-advocacy organizations (Patient Advocate Foundation, EveryLife Foundation, AE Alliance); nothing EEF1A2-specific.
