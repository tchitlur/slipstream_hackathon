# Patient organization gaps (searched 2026-10-03, no organization confirmed)

Genes from `genes.txt` for which no patient organization's own site could be confirmed. One line per gene with what was searched.

- SCN3A — searched "SCN3A patient foundation official website" and "SCN3A foundation OR alliance families nonprofit"; results only returned SCN2A organizations and the Broad SCN-Portal (research tool, not a patient org).
- SCN1B — searched "SCN1B patient foundation official website"; only Simons Searchlight (research program) and Dravet Syndrome Foundation genetics pages; no SCN1B-specific org.
- KCNQ3 — searched "KCNQ3 patient foundation official website" and "site:kcnq2cure.org KCNQ3"; KCNQ2 Cure Alliance and Jack Pribaz Foundation sites focus on KCNQ2 and neither retrieved page states it serves KCNQ3 families (a Cambridge Elements title mentions KCNQ3). RIKEE (rikee.org) is a Baylor research database, not a patient org.
- HCN1 — searched "HCN1 epilepsy patient foundation official website" and "HCN1 Foundation OR HCN1 Epilepsy Foundation"; only general epilepsy foundations and journal articles.
- STX1B — searched "STX1B patient foundation official website"; results were all STXBP1 organizations.
- SYN1 — searched "SYN1 gene patient foundation official website"; results were all SYNGAP1 organizations.
- ARX — searched "ARX Foundation patient organization official website" and "ARX gene foundation families nonprofit epilepsy"; only pharmacy/health-tech companies named ARx and medical references.
- ALDH7A1 — searched "ALDH7A1 pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy patient foundation official website"; found pdeonline.org (PDE Consortium, international registry) but the site describes itself as a clinician/researcher consortium, not a patient/family organization, so it was excluded.
- PNPO — searched "PNPO deficiency patient foundation official website"; only OMIM/MedlinePlus/GeneReviews references.
- DEPDC5 — searched "DEPDC5 patient foundation official website" and "DEPDC5 nonprofit family foundation alliance OR foundation OR cure"; only Child Neurology Foundation / Defeating Epilepsy Foundation disease pages.
- EEF1A2 — searched "EEF1A2 foundation patient organization official website" and "EEF1A2 foundation OR nonprofit families"; no EEF1A2-specific organization found.

## Excluded after review (not gaps, but noted for the panel)
- SYNGAP1 Foundation (syngap1foundation.org) — site states it is "Permanently Closed"; CURE SYNGAP1 is included instead.
- PDE Consortium (pdeonline.org) — clinician/researcher consortium (see ALDH7A1 above).
- sptan1.org — could not confirm it is an organization (Hope for SPTAN1 included instead).
